# Product specification

## Promise

BusiOS AI gives a small-business owner one trusted WhatsApp contact that understands the whole business and coordinates an AI workforce. It turns fragmented signals into one prioritized action, not another dashboard full of alerts.

## Initial customer

The first recommended vertical is appointment-led local service businesses—barbers, salons, dental offices, and home-service contractors—because demand, capacity, follow-up, reputation, and revenue form a measurable closed loop.

## MVP success metrics

- Onboarding completion rate ≥ 70%
- Median time to seeded Business Brain < 5 minutes
- Opportunity approval or modification rate ≥ 25%
- Recommendation-to-measured-result coverage ≥ 80%
- Zero cross-tenant data disclosures
- Zero live consequential actions without recorded approval

## WhatsApp commands

- Normal message: ask Diego a business question.
- `signals: ...`: request a structured cross-functional opportunity.
- `APPROVE`: authorize the pending plan (simulation mode in the MVP).
- Natural-language feedback: ask Diego to revise the plan.

## Commercial pilot

Run a 30-day design-partner pilot with 3–5 businesses in one vertical. Establish baselines for missed inquiries, off-peak utilization, lead response time, weekly revenue, and review sentiment. Measure lift against each approved recommendation and interview owners weekly.

## Roadmap

1. **Foundation:** WhatsApp onboarding, Business Brain, opportunity cards, approval ledger.
2. **Connected signals:** calendar, payments, reviews, CRM, accounting, and CSV ingestion.
3. **Verified execution:** campaigns, follow-up, booking rules, invoice reminders.
4. **Learning loop:** experiment measurement, causal confidence, and playbook reuse with privacy-safe aggregation.
5. **Multi-location OS:** roles, policies, benchmarking, and partner/reseller administration.
